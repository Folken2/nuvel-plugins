# Recipe categories

A fixed vocabulary owned by nuvel. Every recipe's `category` (in its `## Stats`
and in what `learn` sends to nuvel) is exactly one identifier from this list.
Pick the closest; use `other` only when nothing fits. Never invent a category:
the list grows with plugin releases, not per user.

The identifiers are in English and never translated; the descriptions are only
to help choose.

- `client-proposal` — preparing a proposal, quote or offer for a client
- `client-onboarding` — welcoming a new client and setting up the work
- `client-followup` — chasing or updating a client after a conversation
- `meeting-prep` — getting ready for a meeting or call
- `meeting-followup` — notes, actions and follow-up after a meeting
- `sales-pipeline` — reviewing leads, deals and next steps
- `partner-relations` — work with partners, suppliers or distributors
- `investor-update` — updates and materials for investors or the board
- `board-meeting` — preparing or following up a board meeting
- `monthly-close` — month-end review of figures, invoices and pending items
- `invoicing` — preparing, checking or chasing invoices and payments
- `budget-planning` — budgets, forecasts and spending decisions
- `hiring` — job descriptions, candidates, interviews and offers
- `team-management` — one-to-ones, team updates, reviews and feedback
- `reporting` — recurring reports and status summaries
- `contract-review` — reading, comparing or tracking contracts and renewals
- `travel-planning` — trips, agendas and logistics
- `event-planning` — organising events, visits or offsites
- `inbox-triage` — sorting, prioritising and answering a backlog of messages
- `document-drafting` — writing letters, memos, announcements or policies
- `research` — gathering and summarising information on a topic
- `weekly-planning` — planning the week, priorities and calendar blocks
- `other` — none of the above
